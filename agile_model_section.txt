3.X Agile Development Model

This project adopted the Agile software development model to guide the design and implementation
of the AgruKrwanda platform. Agile is an iterative and incremental approach that emphasizes
flexibility, collaboration, and continuous delivery of working software.

WHY AGILE?

Given the complexity of AgruKrwanda — involving multiple user roles (Admin, Cooperative Manager,
Farmer, Buyer), real-time market pricing, AI predictions, and inventory management — Agile was
chosen because:

- Requirements could evolve as stakeholder feedback was gathered
- Features could be developed and tested in short sprints
- Each module (auth, marketplace, harvests, reports) could be delivered independently
- Issues could be identified and fixed early without affecting the entire system

SPRINTS OVERVIEW

Sprint 1 | Foundation       | Database schema (28 tables), MVC structure, authentication
Sprint 2 | Core Roles       | Admin dashboard, Farmer harvest recording, Buyer marketplace
Sprint 3 | Cooperative      | Cooperative manager module, inventory, production plans
Sprint 4 | Intelligence     | AI crop prediction service, market price trends, Chart.js dashboards
Sprint 5 | Polish           | Reports, notifications, audit logging, UI refinement

AGILE PRACTICES APPLIED

- Iterative development with testable increments per sprint
- Continuous integration of modules into the MVC framework
- Regular self-review and refinement of features
- Backlog prioritization based on user role importance
